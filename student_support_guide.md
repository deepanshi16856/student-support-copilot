# Student Support Guide

These are sample guidelines for the Student Support Copilot demonstration, not official institutional policies.

## KB001 — Course and Batch Access

Verify the student's account, payment, and enrollment status. If the batch remains unavailable, escalate to support. Never claim access has been restored without verification.

## KB002 — Payment Issues

Ask the student to check their transaction status and retain the transaction reference. Support must verify payment before confirming its status. Escalate duplicate deductions and unresolved transactions. Never request passwords or OTPs.

## KB003 — Refund Requests

Collect the order or transaction reference and route the request to the authorised support team. Do not promise refunds or processing times without verified policy information.

## KB004 — Account and Login Issues

Confirm the registered account and suggest the official password-reset process. Never request passwords or OTPs. Escalate persistent access issues.

## KB005 — Academic Support

Identify the subject, topic, and specific question. Direct the student to relevant course lessons or the available academic support channel.

## KB006 — Technical Problems

Record the device, browser or app, and error details. Suggest restarting or updating the app when appropriate. Escalate persistent issues.

## Human Escalation Rules

Escalate high-priority tickets, refund requests, disputed payments, complaints, suspected fraud, legal threats, unresolved issues, and tickets without a relevant knowledge-base article. Drafted replies should be reviewed by a human before being sent.
